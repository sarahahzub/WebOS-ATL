webOS ATL runs Android apps natively on rooted LG webOS TVs, using Android Translation Layer (no emulator, no Android VM).

Requirements: rooted TV with Homebrew Channel, 64-bit CPU (uname -m = aarch64), 600 MB free space, 1.2 GB RAM. Tested on LG 43UR7500 (webOS 23); other models are untested.

After installing, open webOS ATL and press Install: it sets up its service and downloads the Android runtime (about 200 MB). Then install APKs from a phone or laptop browser at http://<tv-ip>:8790/, or from a download link in the app. Each Android app gets its own icon in the TV's app list.

Many apps work, many do not yet (no Google Play services, limited WebView and media support). See the project page for the compatibility list and known issues.
