A desktop Chromium build for the TV, driven with the remote: arrows move a pointer, OK clicks, CH+/CH- scroll, BACK goes back. Text fields open the TV keyboard.

Needs webOS ATL: after this app is installed, the webOS ATL service downloads the browser image (about 160 MB) within a minute. Open webOS ATL to see the progress.

Known issues: the three-dot menu does not open, and full-screen video may close the browser.
