webOS ATL

 
Runs Android apps on this TV. Setup copies the programs to /media/developer/webosatl, adds a start-up hook
 (Homebrew Channel) and then downloads the Android runtime (about 200 MB) in the background.

 
  • Needs a rooted TV with Homebrew Channel, a 64-bit (aarch64) CPU and 600 MB free space
    • 
 
  • 1.5 GB RAM or more recommended; tested on LG 43UR7500 (webOS 23)
    • 
 
  • Remove it any time: Uninstall in this app, then remove the app in Homebrew Channel
